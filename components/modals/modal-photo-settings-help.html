lightbulb Photo Quality Settings Recommendation

A 320x320 size with 0.3 quality will indeed be "very blurry". If you just want to take an abstract picture like "this is a cup of coffee," it might barely suffice. However, if you are capturing a "physical invoice or receipt," the text and numbers on it will definitely blur together and become illegible.

To maintain the legibility of receipt numbers, we recommend setting it to at least 480x480 / Quality 0.5.

Estimated Single Photo Size (After Saving)
  • 320x320 / Quality 0.3: Approx. 10~20 KB (Very small but extremely blurry)
  • 480x480 / Quality 0.5: Approx. 15~30 KB
  • 640x640 / Quality 0.7: Approx. 40~60 KB (Default, good clarity)
  • 1024x1024 / Quality 0.9: Approx. 150~250 KB (Extremely clear but larger size)
